About these numbers

  • Views: how many times posts, stories and reels were shown, counting repeats.
  • Viewers: unique accounts that saw anything. For 7 and 30 days this comes straight from Instagram. For longer ranges it's daily numbers added up, so people get counted more than once (marked ≈).
  • Interactions: likes, comments, shares, saves and reposts.
  • Net followers: follows minus unfollows. Follower counts before tracking started are worked back from these.
  • Audience: age, gender and city of current followers, as reported by Instagram this month.
  • LinkedIn: company page stats come from LinkedIn's API daily: impressions, unique impressions, likes, comments, reposts, clicks, new followers and page views. If the API isn't connected, they're entered by hand each week instead.

Instagram updates automatically every morning. Stories are saved once a day while they're live.
